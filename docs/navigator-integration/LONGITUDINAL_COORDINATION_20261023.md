Local paired integration, October 23, 2026. Default off. No change to the retained angle controller, 1.20/0.97 mapping, vehicle geometry, Ki0.5, longitudinal actuator delay, safety limits or firmware. This owner-specific research feature applies only to the forced Expedition/Navigator platform with openpilot longitudinal enabled and the parent's explicit `FordPrefLongitudinalCoordination` opt-in. It is not enabled by selecting Alpha alone.

A fault immediately neutralizes this controller's longitudinal packet and stays latched while the owner remains enabled. The parent inhibits longitudinal control and resets LongControl; selfdrived uses the normal `controlsMismatch` immediate-disable/no-entry alert. Fresh data or a pedal override cannot silently rearm. Detailed cause is recorded in feedback; there is no claim of an ECU fault. Steering requests remain under the unchanged existing controller until normal selfdrived disengagement.
